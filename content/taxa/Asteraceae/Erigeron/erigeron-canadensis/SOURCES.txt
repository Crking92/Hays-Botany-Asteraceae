HORSEWEED — accepted Erigeron canadensis L.; Conyza canadensis (L.) Cronquist is synonym; do not make two cards.
Kew accepted taxonomy: https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:203653-1 ; Kew Conyza synonym: https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/64815-2
Flora of North America Conyza/Erigeron treatment copied in World Flora Online: https://www.worldfloraonline.org/taxon/wfo-0000129910
POWO range native Texas; project-zone 3-county evidence not audited. Master Plant Pool row 431 Erigeron canadensis approved curated Yes (own observations 9 incl 8 wild), row 292 Conyza canadensis Hays Local Yes but uncurated. These map to one Erigeron species card and alias.

Whole flowering horseweed: upright central stem, MANY narrow alternate stem leaves, upper flowering pyramid of numerous tiny heads. Historic taxon synonym Conyza on page; same species.
3 03-tiny-flower-heads.jpg — Rasbak, CC BY-SA 3.0 — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Conyza_canadensis_flowerhead%2C_Canadese_fijnstraal_bloemhoofdje_%285%29.jpg — direct Special:Redirect/file/Conyza%20canadensis%20flowerhead%2C%20Canadese%20fijnstraal%20bloemhoofdje%20(5).jpg. Tiny heads are typically ~3–4 mm long with very short white to pinkish threadlike rays; this does NOT resemble a large fleabane daisy. Zoom to see the head/involucre rather than assume it's still a green bud.
4 04-mature-pappus-seeds.jpg — Rasbak, CC BY-SA 3.0 — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Conyza_canadensis_seeds%2C_Canadese_fijnstraal_zaden.jpg — direct Special:Redirect/file/Conyza%20canadensis%20seeds%2C%20Canadese%20fijnstraal%20zaden.jpg. Mature fruiting heads with silky white PAPPUS: the bristles spread small dry cypselae by wind; fruit is not a 'dandelion seed' or a single flower.
