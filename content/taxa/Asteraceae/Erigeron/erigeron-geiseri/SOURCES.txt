Erigeron geiseri Shinners, Geiser's fleabane. Approved Master Plant Pool row 432, reviewed 2026-10-11.
Taxonomy: Kew POWO https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:93795-2 ; Flora of North America through WFO https://www.worldfloraonline.org/taxon/wfo-0000038921 .
Identification: slender annual taproot; lower spreading or deflexed stem hairs; upper strigose hairs; 44-70 rays (3-6 mm); involucre 2.5-3.5 mm. Check root crown and hairs, not rays alone.
Compare Erigeron modestus, E. tenellus and E. versicolor. Historical E. geiseri var. calcicola maps to E. versicolor, not a synonym to merge into this card.
