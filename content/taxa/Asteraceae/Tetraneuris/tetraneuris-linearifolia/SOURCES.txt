## Morphology
UT Plant Resources Center, Vascular Plants of Williamson County — Tetraneuris linearifolia var. linearifolia:
https://w3.biosci.utexas.edu/prc/K12/pdf/Tetraneuris%20linearifolia%20linearifolia

Key learner cues:
- annual / taprooted
- narrow linear foliage
- hairy/gland-dotted foliage
- solitary yellow radiate heads
- distinct multi-series phyllaries
- scale-like pappus
