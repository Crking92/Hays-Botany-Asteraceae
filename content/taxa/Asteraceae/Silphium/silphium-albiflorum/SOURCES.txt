Silphium albiflorum — B129 LEARNER FAST-FILL

Science
- Current Kew/POWO accepts Silphium albiflorum A.Gray and treats it as Texas-native.
- BONAP/NAPA is the project county-distribution cross-check.
- Travis County: University of Texas Plant Resources Center specimen/index evidence.
- Burnet County: documented occurrence at Doeskin Ranch, Balcones Canyonlands National Wildlife Refuge.
- Mason County: Texas Parks and Wildlife Department Mason Mountain WMA vascular flora.
- Project decision: PASS — Travis + Burnet + Mason.
- Guardrail: the Purola/Hays occurrence is introduced and is not counted as native Hays evidence.

Identification
- White ray florets are the fastest project-level split from the yellow-headed Silphium comparisons.
- Foliage is tough/rough and commonly strongly lobed or divided.
- Dry limestone/calcareous prairie is characteristic habitat.
