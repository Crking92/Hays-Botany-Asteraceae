## Frostweed scientific/visual polish — 2026-10-13
Current FNA and UT Williamson plant descriptions; previous content preserved where not contradicted.

Critical diagnostic:
- Verbesina virginica perennial 1–2m or more; large green stems with WAVY thin decurrent wings from tapering leaf bases (UT Williamson 3–5.5mm wing width); short pubescent stems, sometimes lower woody base.
- Leaves typically ALTERNATE (occasionally opposite in lowest nodes), scabrous, large variably toothed simple blade with base tapering down stem. Wings are STEM wings, distinct from papery FRUIT wings.
- Terminal canopy can hold 60–100+ small white composite heads; each head typically 1–3 small white ray florets and 8–13 whitish disc florets in regional plants.
- Mature flattened cypselae conspicuously WINGED and have TWO erect persistent short (1.5–3mm) pappus AWNS. The old species.json claim "pappus reduced or absent" was wrong and is corrected.
- "Frost flowers" are transient ice ribbons formed by water exiting fissures in the lower stem and freezing during suitable first freezes. Not a botanical flower, and absence of ice does not exclude frostweed.
